What you can do

Log your valuesPick the test date and type in the numbers from your report. Add a note about how you were doing that day.
Trend chartsSee each item over one week up to one year, or any date range you choose.
Reference rangesSet a minimum and maximum for each item and the range is drawn on the chart.
Only the items you needChoose from common tests such as CBC, liver, kidney, glucose and lipids, or add your own.
Backup and restoreExport your records to a file for safekeeping and load them on a new device.
Install like an appAdd it to your home screen. It opens like an app and works without a connection.

Who it helps

  • People in chemotherapy who follow white cells, neutrophils or platelets from cycle to cycle
  • People with liver or kidney conditions or diabetes who have regular blood work
  • Caregivers keeping a family member's results in order
  • Anyone who wants to compare yearly check-up results

Your records stay on your device

The values and notes you enter are saved only in the browser on the device you are using. They are not sent to a server, and there is no account or login.

Because nothing is kept on a server, your records can be lost if you clear your browser data or change devices. Use Back up in Settings to save a file from time to time.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free to use.

What happens to my records if I change phones?

Records live on the device, so they do not move automatically. Use “Back up” on the old device to create a file, then “Restore” on the new one.

Where do the suggested reference ranges come from?

Only some items come with a value from a public source (Korea’s national health screening criteria and guidance from institutes of the U.S. National Institutes of Health). The rest are left blank, and the sources are listed on the Items screen of the app. Ranges and units differ between labs and countries, so enter or edit each item to match the range printed on your own report.

A value is outside the range. What should I do?

This app helps you record and review your numbers. It does not interpret them. Always discuss your results with your own doctor or care team.

Notice

My Blood Diary is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a substitute for professional medical judgment.
