Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Blood Test Log (the “service”) is built so that your test records are not collected or kept on a server. This page explains what information the service handles and how.

1. Personal information we collect

The service has no sign-up or login. It does not collect any personal information, including your name, contact details or test values, on the operator's servers.

2. Information stored on your device

The following information you enter is saved only in the browser storage (localStorage) of the device you are using.

  • Test dates, test values and notes
  • The names, units and reference ranges of your items
  • Your appearance and language settings

This information is not sent to the operator or to any third party, and the operator cannot see it.

3. Deleting and backing up

  • Tapping “Delete all data” in Settings, or clearing the site data in your browser, deletes the records stored on your device.
  • A file created with “Back up” is saved on your device. Keeping it safe is up to you.
  • Because records are not on a server, the operator cannot recover records that are deleted or lost.

4. Access logs

The service's web pages are delivered through a third-party hosting provider. When a page is loaded, the hosting provider may process access logs such as IP address, time of access and browser type for security and operational purposes. These logs do not include the test records you enter.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The service does not currently use analytics tools or advertising, and it does not set tracking cookies. If analytics or advertising is introduced later, this policy will be updated first to explain what information is processed and how. Even then, the test records stored on your device will not be transmitted.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text and its effective date will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, write to jeongtaeyop@naver.com.
